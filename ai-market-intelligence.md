# AI Market Intelligence

Information asymmetry is the biggest barrier to entry in the carbon economy. CRBN Credit leverages artificial intelligence to level the playing field, providing our users with actionable, real-time intelligence.

## The Live Intel Feed

Our terminal features a continuous **Live Intel Feed** that acts as an AI strategy memory. 
- It aggregates news from regulatory bodies, scientific journals, and financial media.
- It translates complex methodology updates (e.g., Verra's consolidated REDD+ methodology changes) into plain-English institutional impact summaries.

## Sentiment Analysis & News Screener

The **News Screener** monitors global news flow and assigns sentiment scores (Bullish, Bearish, Neutral) to relevant carbon market events. 
For example, if a sovereign nation announces a ban on carbon credit exports under Article 6, the AI immediately flags the regulatory risk and highlights which portfolio assets are exposed in that jurisdiction.

## AI Investment Strategist

Users can interface with our AI Investment Strategist to generate bespoke investment theses.
- **Inputs**: User defines risk profile (Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive), budget, and target sector (e.g., Tech-Based Removals).
- **Outputs**: The system generates a comprehensive 2026-2030 investment thesis, complete with suggested asset allocation, projected yields, and identified risk factors.
