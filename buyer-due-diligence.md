# Buyer Due Diligence Guide

Due diligence in the carbon market requires analyzing physical risk, methodology risk, and financial risk. CRBN Credit’s terminal automates much of this, but buyers should understand the underlying framework.

## Step 1: Methodology Review
Carbon credits are only as good as the methodology under which they were generated. 
- *Red Flags*: Avoid legacy renewable energy methodologies (e.g., building solar in developed nations) as they no longer pass the financial additionality test.
- *Green Flags*: Look for stringent, recently updated methodologies (e.g., Verra's new consolidated REDD+ methodology VM0048).

## Step 2: Spatial and Physical Risk Analysis
For forestry and land-use projects, desk research is insufficient. 
- Use the **CRBN Credit Risk Heatmap** to check historical wildfire patterns over the project coordinates.
- Verify if the project's deforestation baseline makes logical sense compared to neighboring regions.

## Step 3: Developer Track Record
Investigate the project developer.
- Have their previous projects faced methodology suspensions?
- Do they have a history of over-crediting?
- CRBN Credit maintains a database of developer ratings to assist with this screening.

## Step 4: Verification Body Assessment
A project is audited by a Verification and Validation Body (VVB). Ensure the VVB is highly rated, accredited by the registry, and hasn't faced recent disciplinary actions.
