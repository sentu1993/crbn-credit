# Carbon Market Landscape: Current State & Future Trajectory

The global carbon market is evolving rapidly, driven by urgent corporate net-zero commitments, regulatory compliance mandates, and technological innovations in carbon removal. As of 2026, the combined value of compliance and voluntary carbon markets exceeds $1.2 Trillion.

## Compliance vs. Voluntary Dynamics

The landscape is primarily split into two distinct arenas:

1. **Compliance Markets (EU ETS, California Cap-and-Trade, China National ETS)**
   - Dominated by government mandates.
   - Characterized by high liquidity, standardized assets (like EUAs), and high prices (often trading above €70/tonne).
   - Expected to tighten further as free allocations are phased out and policies like the EU's "Fit for 55" accelerate.

2. **The Voluntary Carbon Market (VCM)**
   - Driven by corporate ESG goals rather than legal mandates.
   - Historically fragmented and plagued by variable quality.
   - Currently undergoing a massive "flight to quality," heavily driven by initiatives like the ICVCM (Integrity Council for the Voluntary Carbon Market).

## Key Players in the Ecosystem

- **Registries**: Verra, Gold Standard, Puro.earth, American Carbon Registry. These entities set the rules (methodologies) and issue credits.
- **Project Developers**: The organizations on the ground building direct air capture plants, planting forests, or deploying biochar.
- **Intermediaries**: Brokers and trading desks that facilitate OTC transactions.
- **Intelligence Platforms**: Infrastructure like **CRBN Credit** that aggregates, normalizes, and prices these disparate assets for institutional consumption.

## Future Trajectory: The "Supply Squeeze"

Our macro analysis indicates a severe supply deficit for high-integrity removal credits beginning in Q4 2026. As Article 6.4 of the Paris Agreement operationalizes, cheap avoidance credits are becoming stranded assets, while verified removals (DAC, Biochar, Enhanced Weathering) are commanding unprecedented premiums.
