# Carbon Price Forecasting (2026–2030)

CRBN Credit provides institutional consensus forecasting for global carbon markets, combining proprietary data with models from leading analysts (EY, McKinsey, etc.).

## The Core Thesis: "The Supply Squeeze"

Our analysis indicates a severe supply deficit for high-integrity removal credits beginning in Q4 2026. As Article 6.4 of the Paris Agreement operationalizes and corporate Net Zero deadlines approach (2030), a rapid market bifurcation is expected:
- **"Junk" offsets** (low-quality legacy avoidance) become stranded assets trending toward $0.
- **Verified Removals** (DAC, Biochar, Enhanced Weathering) command significant, escalating premiums.

### Analysis by Asset Class

**1. EU ETS (Compliance)**
Prices are heavily supported by the aggressive reduction of the EU's emission cap and the phase-out of free allowances under the CBAM transition. Expect sustained upward pressure toward €145+ by 2030.

**2. Tech-Based Removals (DAC, Biochar)**
Currently constrained by immense capital expenditure and high Levelized Cost of Capture (LCOC). As technology scales and massive government subsidies (like the US IRA) take effect, the cost per tonne will decrease by 2030, though demand will vastly outstrip supply, keeping prices firmly above $150.

**3. Nature-Based Removals (Reforestation, Blue Carbon)**
Prices will double or triple by 2030 as the market recognizes the scarcity of available land for high-quality afforestation and the increasing physical risks (wildfires) threatening existing stock.

**4. Legacy Avoidance**
Unverified renewable energy projects from the 2010s possess zero additionality today. These assets will become entirely obsolete.
