# Carbon Risk Framework

Investing in carbon credits carries unique physical, regulatory, and reputational risks. CRBN Credit provides a comprehensive risk framework to protect institutional portfolios.

## 1. Physical Reversal Risk

For nature-based solutions (NBS), carbon stored in forests or soils can be released back into the atmosphere due to:
- Wildfires
- Disease or pest outbreaks
- Illegal logging

*Mitigation Tools*: CRBN Credit features a **Risk Heatmap** utilizing geospatial satellite analysis to map fire, drought, and deforestation risks over project boundaries. We also analyze the registry's **Buffer Pool** health.

## 2. Regulatory & Baseline Invalidation Risk

Carbon methodologies are constantly evolving. A project considered high-quality in 2018 might be invalidated today if its baseline (the estimate of what would have happened without the project) is deemed exaggerated.
- *Mitigation Tools*: Our **Project Auditor** tracks methodology updates and flags credits generated under deprecated or scrutinized methodologies.

## 3. Reputational Risk (Greenwashing)

Corporate buyers face severe backlash if they offset emissions using "junk" credits.
- *Mitigation Tools*: Alignment with the ICVCM Core Carbon Principles (CCPs). We filter and flag assets that do not meet rigorous additionality and permanence standards, ensuring your Net Zero claims withstand public and auditor scrutiny.
