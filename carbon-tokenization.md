# Carbon Tokenization and On-Chain Infrastructure

Tokenization is revolutionizing carbon markets by bridging traditional carbon credits to blockchain infrastructure. This provides 24/7 trading, instant settlement, and radically transparent pricing.

## What is Tokenized Carbon?

A tokenized carbon credit is a digital representation of a verified carbon offset on a public blockchain (typically Polygon, Celo, or Ethereum). 
When a credit is tokenized, it is usually "bridged" from a traditional registry (like Verra) via a two-way or one-way bridge (like Toucan Protocol).

### Key Tokens in the Ecosystem
- **BCT (Base Carbon Tonne)**: A pool token representing standard VCM credits.
- **NCT (Nature Carbon Tonne)**: A pool token specifically for nature-based solutions.
- **KLIMA**: The reserve currency of KlimaDAO, backed by on-chain carbon tokens.

## Benefits of On-Chain Carbon

1. **Liquidity**: Traditional carbon credits are often traded OTC via brokers with high fees and slow settlement. On-chain Automated Market Makers (AMMs) like Uniswap provide instant, deep liquidity.
2. **Transparency**: Every transaction, retirement, and price movement is visible on the public ledger.
3. **Programmability**: Smart contracts enable "Regenerative Finance" (ReFi) applications, allowing automated carbon offsetting for businesses and dApps.
