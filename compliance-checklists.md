# Compliance Checklists for Institutional Buyers

Navigating the carbon market requires rigorous adherence to evolving standards to prevent reputational damage (greenwashing) and ensure regulatory compliance. Use these checklists before finalizing any carbon asset procurement.

## 1. Environmental Integrity Checklist (ICVCM Alignment)
Ensure the carbon project meets the Core Carbon Principles (CCPs):
- [ ] **Additionality Verified**: Is there clear proof the project required carbon finance to exist?
- [ ] **Robust Quantification**: Is the baseline scenario conservative and backed by third-party data?
- [ ] **No Double Counting**: Has the registry confirmed exclusive issuance and prevented double claiming?
- [ ] **Permanence Assessed**: If NBS (Nature-Based Solution), is there an adequate buffer pool allocation?
- [ ] **Sustainable Development**: Does the project negatively impact local communities or biodiversity?

## 2. Regulatory Disclosure Checklist (CSRD & SEC)
If retiring credits for corporate reporting:
- [ ] **Vintage Alignment**: Does the credit vintage match or precede the emission year being offset?
- [ ] **Scope Match**: Are you correctly applying the offset to Scope 1, 2, or 3 as per your internal policy?
- [ ] **Registry Certificate**: Do you possess the cryptographically secure proof-of-offset or registry PDF?
- [ ] **Audit Trail**: Is the methodology and verification report attached to the retirement ledger?

## 3. Financial & Legal Checklist
- [ ] **KYC/AML Cleared**: Has the counterparty (broker or developer) cleared institutional compliance?
- [ ] **Fair Value Check**: Does the purchase price align with CRBN Credit's 30-day VWAP Fair Value curve?
- [ ] **Jurisdictional Risk**: Is the project located in a country proposing Article 6 export bans?
