# AI-Driven Fair Value Model

Beyond our standard pricing methodology, CRBN Credit employs an advanced AI-Driven Fair Value Model to estimate prices for highly illiquid or bespoke carbon projects that do not have active daily trading volume.

## Model Inputs

When a project lacks recent trade data, our machine learning models extrapolate a fair value based on highly correlated proxy assets. The model analyzes:

1. **Methodology Premium/Discount**: E.g., Biochar vs. REDD+.
2. **Vintage Decay**: Older vintages (e.g., >5 years old) are automatically discounted based on current market preference curves, as buyers overwhelmingly favor recent emission reductions.
3. **Geopolitical Risk Premium**: Projects in stable jurisdictions command premiums over those in volatile regions due to lower expropriation or reversal risks.
4. **SDG Co-Benefit Multipliers**: Projects boasting multiple UN Sustainable Development Goals (e.g., clean water, gender equality) receive a calculated premium based on historical OTC data.

## Rating Synthesis

The Fair Value model is deeply integrated with our **Rating Analyzer**. We aggregate quality ratings from leading rating agencies (like Sylvera, BeZero, and Calyx Global) into a composite Carbon Credit Quality Score. 

Credits with composite AAA ratings are automatically adjusted upward in our fair value curves, reflecting the "flight to quality" currently dominating institutional buying behavior.
