# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What does CRBN.credit do?
CRBN.credit provides market intelligence, analysis, and research tools focused on global carbon markets, including voluntary and compliance systems. We act as the central nervous system for institutional carbon data.

### Is CRBN.credit a trading platform?
No. CRBN.credit does not act as a market maker, execute trades, or sell proprietary carbon credits. We focus purely on data aggregation, pricing insights, and providing a neutral decision support terminal.

### Who is the platform built for?
The platform is designed for institutions, corporate sustainability desks, brokers, analysts, hedge funds, consultants, and advanced market participants seeking structured carbon market insights.

### Does CRBN.credit provide financial advice?
No. All content on the platform is informational and analytical in nature and should not be considered financial or investment advice.

### What markets does CRBN.credit cover?
Our coverage includes the Voluntary Carbon Market (VCM), compliance systems such as the EU ETS, on-chain tokenized carbon (ReFi), and emerging regulatory frameworks like CBAM, CORSIA, and Article 6.

### How is data sourced?
Data is aggregated from public registries (Verra, Gold Standard, Puro.earth), regulatory disclosures, centralized and decentralized exchange feeds, and structured research inputs.

### Is AI used on the platform?
Yes. AI is used extensively to assist with research synthesis, fair value modeling, news sentiment analysis, and the structured interpretation of complex methodology updates.

### Is CRBN.credit affiliated with any registry?
No. The platform operates independently and does not represent any registry, exchange, or issuing body.

### What is the long-term vision?
The long-term goal is to become the trusted intelligence layer for global carbon markets, supporting transparency, accountability, and informed participation as the market matures toward a multi-trillion-dollar scale.
