# Institutional Use Cases

CRBN Credit provides the infrastructure for diverse institutional participants to interact with the carbon economy safely and efficiently.

## 1. Corporate Treasuries & Sustainability Desks
**The Challenge**: A Fortune 500 company has committed to Net Zero by 2030 but lacks the expertise to procure high-quality carbon removals without risking greenwashing PR disasters.
**The Solution**: Utilizing the **Portfolio Architect Pro**, the treasury allocates an annual budget. CRBN Credit automatically constructs a risk-adjusted portfolio (e.g., 50% Biochar, 50% Blue Carbon), executes the OTC trades securely, and generates audit-ready retirement certificates for their SEC disclosures.

## 2. Hedge Funds & Asset Managers
**The Challenge**: Generating alpha from the anticipated "Supply Squeeze" in removal credits without taking on bespoke project failure risk.
**The Solution**: Using the **Market Data API** and **Websocket Feeds**, quantitative funds build algorithmic trading strategies across tokenized carbon DEXs and traditional CEXs, arbitraging price discrepancies and holding high-quality assets as a macro hedge.

## 3. Importers & Supply Chain Managers (CBAM)
**The Challenge**: An EU-based steel importer faces massive new carbon taxes under the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) and needs to optimize supply chain routing.
**The Solution**: The **Compliance Navigator** provides live differential tracking between EU ETS allowance prices and origin-country carbon costs, allowing the importer to optimize procurement routes dynamically.

## 4. Web3 / ReFi Developers
**The Challenge**: Building an application that automatically offsets the carbon footprint of blockchain transactions.
**The Solution**: Integrating the CRBN Credit **Retirement API** allows the dApp to programmatically retire tokenized carbon in the background, providing users with instant cryptographic proof of their environmental impact.
