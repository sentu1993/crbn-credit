# The Market Fragmentation Problem

Information asymmetry is the biggest barrier to entry in the modern carbon economy. Unlike traditional financial markets where equities are traded on centralized exchanges with universally accepted standards, the carbon market is highly fragmented.

## The Scope of Fragmentation

1. **Registry Silos**: The VCM relies on independent registries (Verra, Gold Standard, Puro.earth). Each registry maintains its own database, methodology taxonomy, and issuance ledger.
2. **Pricing Disconnects**: Carbon credits are not fungible commodities. A 2021 Verra REDD+ credit trades at a completely different price than a 2024 Puro.earth Biochar credit. Prices across Centralized Exchanges (CEXs), Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs), and Over-The-Counter (OTC) broker networks can vary by over 30% for the exact same project on the exact same day.
3. **Data Opaqueness**: Historically, finding the fair market value of a specific project required calling multiple OTC brokers to piece together an order book.

## Market Consequences

- **Illiquidity**: Buyers hesitate to purchase assets when they cannot confidently determine a fair mark-to-market price.
- **Greenwashing Risks**: Without unified quality data, corporations accidentally purchase low-quality credits that expose them to reputational damage.
- **Capital Inefficiency**: Billions of dollars earmarked for climate finance remain deployed inefficiently due to a lack of institutional-grade infrastructure.

## The CRBN Credit Solution

CRBN Credit solves this fragmentation by acting as the **Central Nervous System of the Carbon Economy**. Our Oracle Engine continuously polls registry APIs, parses issuance PDFs, monitors on-chain ledger events, and aggregates price feeds to provide a single, unified view of global carbon supply and demand.
