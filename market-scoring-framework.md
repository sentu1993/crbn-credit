# Market Scoring Framework

Beyond evaluating the environmental integrity of a specific carbon project, institutional traders need to evaluate the *market viability* of the asset. The CRBN Credit Market Scoring Framework provides deep liquidity and volatility metrics.

## The Composite Market Score

Our framework generates a score (0 to 100) indicating how easily an institutional position can be entered or exited. The score evaluates:

### 1. Liquidity Depth (40%)
- Analysis of 30-day average daily trading volume across CEXs and DEXs.
- Identification of active OTC order book depth for the specific project or methodology type.

### 2. Volatility (30%)
- Historical price variance over 90-day and 1-year periods.
- Assets with massive price swings (e.g., tokenized carbon during crypto market fluctuations) receive lower stability scores, alerting conservative corporate treasuries to hedging risks.

### 3. Bid-Ask Spread (20%)
- A tight bid-ask spread indicates a healthy, mature asset. Wide spreads (often seen in highly bespoke, low-volume forestry projects) severely lower the market score.

### 4. Venue Availability (10%)
- Is the asset available on multiple exchanges, or restricted to a single bespoke OTC broker? Multi-venue assets score higher due to reduced counterparty risk.

## Application

Corporate treasurers use the Market Score to ensure their carbon portfolio isn't locked in highly illiquid assets. A high Environmental Quality Rating combined with a high Market Score represents the gold standard for institutional investment.
