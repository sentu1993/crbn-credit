# CRBN Credit Platform Overview

CRBN Credit stands as the central nervous system of the carbon economy. Our platform is engineered to solve the systemic fragmentation across the Voluntary Carbon Market (VCM) and the EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS) by aggregating real-time data across registries, exchanges, and blockchains into a single, unified interface.

## Institutional Grade Infrastructure

Designed for hedge funds, corporate sustainability desks, brokers, and climate-tech innovators, CRBN Credit provides an uncompromised institutional toolset. The global carbon market is scaling rapidly, representing over $1.2 Trillion in compliance and voluntary assets. We ensure you have the data fidelity, execution pathways, and compliance tools necessary to navigate it.

### Core Modules

1. **Portfolio Architect Pro**: An AI-driven allocation engine that generates bespoke portfolio constructions based on risk profiles, budgets, and net-zero targets.
2. **Net Zero Strategy Generator**: Automated reduction pathway builders to align corporate strategies with reporting deadlines.
3. **Live Intel Feed**: A real-time AI strategy memory and news aggregation system.
4. **Project Auditor**: An automated due diligence engine assessing project quality, additionality, permanence, and registry compliance.

## The Fragmentation Problem Solved

Carbon markets suffer from fragmented registries (Verra, Gold Standard, Puro.earth) and disconnected pricing venues. CRBN Credit solves this by routing data through an Oracle Engine that ingests off-chain registry PDFs, API endpoints, and on-chain ReFi ledger data.
