# Portfolio Architecture Pro

Building a resilient, Net Zero-aligned carbon portfolio requires balancing budget constraints with credit integrity and long-term price appreciation. CRBN Credit's **Portfolio Architect Pro** automates this complex process.

## Strategic Allocation Principles

A modern carbon portfolio should never rely on a single project type. We recommend a phased approach in alignment with the Oxford Principles for Net Zero Aligned Carbon Offsetting:

1. **Short-Term (2026-2028)**: A mix of high-quality nature-based avoidance (to protect critical ecosystems) and nature-based removals (afforestation, soil carbon).
2. **Medium-Term (2028-2030)**: Transitioning heavily toward long-lived removals.
3. **Long-Term (2030+)**: 100% permanent engineered removals (DAC, Biochar, Enhanced Weathering).

## How the Architect Works

You input your parameters:
- Total tonnage required to offset.
- Capital budget.
- Corporate ESG goals (e.g., emphasis on biodiversity or local community impact).

The Engine generates an optimized allocation matrix. For example:
- **40% Core Position**: Verified Blue Carbon Removals (High SDG impact, moderate permanence).
- **40% Yield/Value**: High-quality Avoided Deforestation (Jurisdictional REDD+).
- **20% Frontier Tech**: Biochar & DAC (Maximum permanence, future-proofing).

The Architect ensures the blended cost per tonne meets your budget while maximizing the composite quality score of the portfolio.
