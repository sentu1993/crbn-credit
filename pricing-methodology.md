# Pricing Methodology

Carbon credits are notoriously difficult to price. Because every project has unique attributes (vintage, methodology, geography, co-benefits), carbon credits act more like real estate than fungible commodities. CRBN Credit provides institutional-grade pricing clarity.

## The Volatility Challenge

Prices across Centralized Exchanges (CEXs), Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs), and Over-The-Counter (OTC) broker networks can vary by over 30% for the exact same project on the exact same day.

## Our Fair Value Calculation

CRBN Credit does not rely on a single source of truth. We calculate a composite **Fair Value** metric to smooth out volatility from low-liquidity venues and provide a robust mark-to-market price.

**The Fair Value Formula:**
`Fair_Value = (VWAP_CEX × 0.4) + (VWAP_DEX × 0.4) + (OTC_Reported × 0.2)`

### Weighting Breakdown
1. **CEX Volume-Weighted Average Price (40%)**: Derived from major exchanges like Xpansiv CBL and CME Group futures.
2. **DEX Volume-Weighted Average Price (40%)**: Derived from on-chain liquidity pools like Uniswap v3 and SushiSwap.
3. **OTC Broker-Reported Prices (20%)**: Sourced from our network of institutional OTC desks to capture large-block, off-exchange transactions.

This methodology ensures that portfolio managers can accurately mark their carbon assets to market for quarterly reporting and risk assessment.
