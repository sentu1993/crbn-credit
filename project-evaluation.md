# Project Evaluation Criteria

When evaluating a carbon project for inclusion in an institutional portfolio, CRBN Credit recommends analyzing the project across three distinct dimensions.

## 1. Carbon Efficacy
The mathematical certainty that a tonne of carbon was removed or avoided.
- **Leakage**: Did the project just shift emissions elsewhere? (e.g., protecting one forest, but loggers moved to the forest next door).
- **Reversibility**: Will the carbon stay out of the atmosphere for 100+ years? Engineered removals score perfectly here; forestry requires deep buffer pool analysis.

## 2. Market Viability
A high-quality project is useless if it cannot be traded or retired efficiently.
- **Liquidity**: Are there active buyers and sellers?
- **Price Discovery**: Is there enough OTC or exchange volume to establish a Fair Value?
- **Registry Support**: Is it listed on a Tier-1 registry (Verra, Gold Standard, Puro)?

## 3. Beyond Carbon (Co-Benefits)
Institutional buyers, particularly in Europe, demand strong ESG narratives alongside carbon math.
- **Biodiversity**: Does the project protect endangered species?
- **Community Impact**: Does revenue flow back to indigenous or local communities?
- **UN SDGs**: How many Sustainable Development Goals does the project verifiably support? (e.g., SDG 5: Gender Equality, SDG 6: Clean Water).
