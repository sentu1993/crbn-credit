# Project Rating Model

Evaluating a carbon project requires deep technical, scientific, and financial analysis. CRBN Credit’s **Rating Analyzer** automates this due diligence, aggregating insights into a composite Carbon Credit Quality Score.

## Core Evaluation Pillars

Our AI-driven model assesses projects across four primary pillars:

### 1. Additionality
Would this project have happened without the revenue from selling carbon credits?
- *Financial Additionality*: Proving the project IRR (Internal Rate of Return) was negative without carbon finance.
- *Regulatory Additionality*: Proving the project wasn't already mandated by local law.

### 2. Permanence
How long will the carbon stay out of the atmosphere?
- **High Permanence**: Direct Air Capture (10,000+ years), Biochar (100-1,000 years).
- **Variable Permanence**: Forestry projects (vulnerable to fires, disease, illegal logging). We factor in the registry's buffer pool health to discount permanence risk.

### 3. Baseline Accuracy
Did the project developer exaggerate the "business-as-usual" scenario to claim more credits?
- We utilize satellite imagery and historical deforestation rates (for REDD+ projects) to back-test baseline claims against reality.

### 4. Co-Benefits (SDGs)
Does the project provide social or environmental benefits beyond carbon reduction?
- Projects mapping to UN Sustainable Development Goals (e.g., clean water access, biodiversity protection, gender equality) receive a premium rating multiplier.

## Rating Synthesis

Rather than acting as a standalone rating agency, CRBN Credit synthesizes data from leading independent bodies (e.g., Sylvera, BeZero, Calyx Global) alongside our own algorithmic assessments. 
The output is a unified grading scale (e.g., AAA to D) that institutional buyers can rely on for rapid screening.
