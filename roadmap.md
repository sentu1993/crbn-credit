# Product Roadmap

CRBN Credit is rapidly evolving to meet the demands of the institutional carbon economy. Below is our strategic roadmap for upcoming features and integrations.

## Q3 2026: Enhanced Regulatory Compliance
- **Full CSRD Reporting Module**: Automated mapping of carbon portfolio metrics directly to the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive formats.
- **CBAM Ledger Integration**: Real-time tracking of embedded supply chain emissions mapped against live EUA prices.

## Q4 2026: Expanded On-Chain Integrations
- **Celo Network Bridge**: Full integration with the Celo ecological ledger to track micro-retirements and mobile-first ReFi applications.
- **Smart Contract OTC Escrow**: Allowing institutional buyers and project developers to execute high-volume OTC trades trustlessly via CRBN Credit smart contracts.

## Q1 2027: Predictive Physical Risk Modeling
- **Live Satellite Telemetry**: Direct integration with climate satellites to provide live biomass tracking and immediate alerts for wildfire risks near portfolio projects.
- **Algorithmic Trading APIs**: Opening execution endpoints for hedge funds to build automated trading strategies across CEX and DEX carbon liquidity pools.

*Note: The roadmap is subject to change based on regulatory shifts in Article 6 and ICVCM guidelines.*
